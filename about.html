The Story

I've Been Doing This
Since I Was Three.

Darren Gooch, on why building things properly has never really been a choice.

1987

The Wood Yard

My parents opened their first premises. I was three years old, running round the yard with my own little hammer. There was no office, no toilets, just timber and a truck. That's genuinely my earliest memory.

2002

Joining Properly

I joined the family business the day I finished school. By then, if you needed a shed or a fence in Norfolk, you already knew to come and see us.

2016

Beams & Braces

I set out on my own to specialise properly, bespoke joinery, timber frames, and composite decking. That's where the Trex partnership started, and where I found the thing I actually wanted to be known for.

2017

TrexPro Platinum

Trex's highest certification, awarded to fewer than 5% of their contractors. It meant learning to build decks the way Trex actually engineers them to be built, not the way most people get away with.

2024

Trex Pro Advisory Council

Invited onto Trex's own advisory council. I've built over 500 composite decks since 2016, including some of the largest curved decks installed anywhere in the UK, and Trex now asks me directly what's actually working on the ground.

Today

What I Actually Believe

I don't think people buy Trex. I think people buy the outdoor space they've been picturing, and Trex happens to be the best material I've found to build it properly. I use UC4-rated joists, joist protection tape, and plastic subframes on every job because I've seen what happens to decks built without them. I don't build cheap decks. I build the ones that are still exactly right in twenty years.

Want to talk it through with me directly?

Message me on WhatsApp, or book a call. I'd genuinely like to hear what you're picturing.
