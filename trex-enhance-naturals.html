The natural-wood look, done properly.

Trex Enhance brings natural woodgrain embossing and an earthy colour palette that sits easily in any garden. It comes in two collections, Naturals and Basics.

It's made from 95% recycled content, reclaimed wood and plastic film, and needs no staining, sealing or annual treatment. Just a wipe-down when needed.

  • 95% recycled

    Every board puts reclaimed wood fibres and used plastic film to work instead of landfill.

  • Grooved edge

    Every board is grooved for hidden fasteners, so the finished deck has no visible screw heads.

  • Fade & stain resistant

    A protective cap resists fading and everyday stains, backed by a 25-year fade and stain warranty.

  • Made for wet winters

    No rot, no splinters and no annual treatment, through wet Norfolk winters.

Questions about Trex Enhance.

How does Enhance differ from Transcend or Signature?

Enhance has natural woodgrain tones and a 25-year fade and stain warranty. Transcend has the deepest woodgrain and richest colours; Signature has a clean, contemporary profile. Both carry a 50-year fade and stain warranty. I'll help you choose based on the look you're after and the garden it's going in.

What's the difference between Naturals and Basics?

They're two colour collections within the same Enhance range. Naturals has four blended woodgrain tones; Basics has two.

Will the screws be visible once it's installed?

No. Every Enhance board is grooved along the edge for hidden fasteners, so the finished surface has no visible screw heads.

Can Enhance be used on a raised deck?

Yes. For any raised structure, the frame underneath has to be engineered properly for the height, and I design that as part of the job.

How do I clean Trex Enhance?

A brush-down and a rinse with warm soapy water is all routine cleaning needs. I'll give you full care instructions at handover.

How long does installation take?

Most residential decks take a few days to a couple of weeks, depending on size and complexity. I'll give you a precise timeline once I've seen the space.
