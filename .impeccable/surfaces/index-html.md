# Surface brief: Homepage (index.html)

Scope: homepage first; the world rolls out to the other 11 pages after sign-off. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a Norfolk or Suffolk homeowner (heritage home, holiday let, waterside garden) picturing a finished outdoor space. Action: Let's Chat, WhatsApp first. Proof: TrexPRO Platinum 2017, Pro Advisory Council 2024, 500+ decks since 2016, curved-deck specialism, UC4 joists, joist tape and plastic subframes, the 13-colour lineup, and the 8 curated real reviews. The user rejected the setting-out drawing world ("lost the old feel") and pinned: same feel, more professional, luxury modern, high end, balanced light and dark.

Unresolved: calm-water.png and rocky-harbor.png are the same file; "Fully Insured & Qualified" is unverified and has been dropped.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The existing Beams & Braces world (bog oak, oiled brass, Fraunces) executed with the restraint of a luxury house: one idea per screen, real photography at full scale, proof stated quietly. It refuses the busy tradesman template: badges, pills, marquees, star rows, floating buttons and card grids.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ink and bog oak, alternating with limewash and sawdust linen. Brass is used like jewellery: hairlines, one italic word, focus rings. Fraunces 300 display at large optical size, with a brass italic accent on dark only. Plus Jakarta Sans body, small and airy. Square-cut buttons, 1px brass or ink hairlines, no shadows, no cards, no radius.

STORY: The visitor feels the finished space, hears Darren's belief in his own words, trusts the quiet credentials, sees the ranges and every real board colour, sees real work, reads a client, then chats.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-viewport real photo (hot tub on a grey Trex deck, raking light) with a deep bog-oak fade from the bottom left. Bottom left: "Not everyone needs a *premium* deck." in large light Fraunces, the brass italic on "premium", Darren's one-line lead, a brass-filled Let's Chat and a hairline WhatsApp button. Along the foot, a brass hairline with one quiet credentials line. The nav is transparent over the photo and turns solid on scroll.

FORM: User-pinned direction (safer register, steered twice: "same feel but more professional, luxury and high end", then "luxury modern"). Seed key 01e00336. Code-led. Signature interaction: the hero photo settles from a slow 1.06 scale on load; section photographs unveil with a clip-path wipe as they enter; the colour gallery crossfades a large square board preview.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
