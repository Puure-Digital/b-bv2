Deep woodgrain. Lasting colour. No annual staining.

Transcend is for homeowners who want the look of premium hardwood without the upkeep: Trex's deepest woodgrain, in five UK colours from warm woodgrain tones to cool coastal greys.

  • Fade & stain resistant

    A protective shell guards against UV, moisture and everyday stains, backed by a 50-year fade and stain warranty.

  • Scratch resistant

    The protective shell resists everyday scratches from furniture, foot traffic and pets.

  • Splinter-free

    No splinters and no rough edges. Comfortable underfoot, including bare feet.

  • Hidden fasteners

    A grooved edge takes hidden fasteners, so the finished surface has no visible screws.

Questions about Trex Transcend.

How does Trex Transcend compare to Trex Enhance?

Transcend has a deeper woodgrain and a wider colour palette, and carries a 50-year fade and stain warranty. Enhance carries 25 years. I'll show you real samples of both.

Does Trex Transcend scratch easily?

No. Its protective shell is designed to resist everyday scratches from furniture, foot traffic and pets.

Can I use Trex Transcend around a pool or hot tub?

Yes, it's a popular choice around water. It won't rot, and I'll plan the layout and drainage around how you'll use the space.

How long does installation take?

Most residential decks take a few days to a couple of weeks, depending on size and complexity. I'll give you a precise timeline once I've seen the space.
