The details that make it feel finished.

A deck is only half the picture. Railing shapes how a space feels, and lighting decides whether you're still out there after the sun's gone down. Both are part of how I plan a deck from the start, not something bolted on at the end.

Let's ChatSee railing and lighting

Trex Signature railing. Part of the deck, not an add-on.

Railing comes up on almost every raised deck I build. It's the first thing you see, and it decides whether a deck looks finished or half-done. As a TrexPRO Platinum installer, I fit Trex Signature aluminium railing, the same brand as the decking, so the whole thing reads as one considered space rather than a deck with a rail bolted on.

  • Same brand as the boards

    Trex railing on a Trex deck, so the rail and the boards read as one piece.

  • Balusters or glass

    Square or round aluminium balusters, or glass panels, with matching aluminium gates.

  • Three matte finishes

    Charcoal Black, Bronze or Classic White. Glass railing comes in Charcoal Black.

  • It curves too

    Signature comes as curvable hand railing, so a curved deck keeps one continuous rail.

  • Built to last

    Powder-coated aluminium that resists fading and corrosion, backed by Trex's 50-year Limited Warranty.

Ellumière deck lighting. What happens after dark.

I fit Ellumière lighting, supplied through Birkdale, mostly spotlights set into step edges, posts and balustrades, planned in with the deck so it looks like part of the build.

  • Spotlights, deck lights and bollards

    Small and large spotlights, deck lights set into the boards and bollard lights, in black or stainless steel.

  • Low voltage

    One outdoor transformer takes 240V mains down to 12V for the whole run of lights.

  • Warm white

    Warm white bulbs: soft light for evenings outside, not a security-light glare.

  • Part of the plan

    Steps, posts and the deck edge worked out with the design, not added once it's built.

The parts.

The lights run off a 12V cable from an outdoor transformer, joined with T-piece connectors, so the layout is planned as one circuit.

How I work, from first chat to handover

Questions about railing and lighting.

Is the railing the same brand as the decking?

Yes. I fit Trex Signature aluminium railing, so the rail and the boards come from the same system. I install the decking and the railing as one job.

Do railing and lighting cost extra?

They're priced alongside the deck from the start, not added on once it's built. Tell me what you're picturing and I'll plan them in.

Can you add railing or lighting to a deck I already have?

Send me a few photos on WhatsApp and I'll tell you honestly what's possible.

Do you build by the water?

Yes. Waterside gardens on the Norfolk Broads and the coast are a big part of the work I do across Norfolk and Suffolk.
