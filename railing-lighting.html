Finishing Touches

The Details That
Make It Feel Finished.

A deck is only half the picture. Railing shapes how a space feels, and lighting decides whether you're still out there after the sun's gone down. Both are things I fit as standard, not optional extras.

Trex Signature Railing

A big part of what we do, not an add-on.

Railing gets asked about on almost every raised deck we build, and for good reason: it's the first thing you see and the thing that decides whether a deck looks finished or half-done. We fit the Trex Signature aluminium system, the same brand as the decking itself, so the whole thing reads as one considered space rather than a deck with a rail bolted on.

  • Horizontal Balustrade

    Aluminium horizontal-rod infill in Charcoal Black, the option we fit most, alongside round or square baluster styles.

  • Glass Railing Kits

    Top and bottom rails grooved for full glass panels, the choice for a raised deck or a view worth keeping unobstructed.

  • Matching Gate

    Adjustable aluminium gate, up to around 48in wide, with a self-closing hinge and lockable hasp, useful wherever the deck meets a pool, pet area, or drop.

  • Genuinely Low Maintenance

    Powder-coated aluminium, not painted timber, so no repainting, no rust streaks, no rotting posts.

Weatherproof
Ellumière Deck Lighting

The other big one: what happens after dark.

I keep Ellumière lighting in stock, supplied through Birkdale, and it's mostly the spotlights I fit into step edges, posts, and balustrades. It's a genuinely good system: a single transformer steps 240V mains down to a safe 12V, so there's no electrician needed and no chasing cable through the house.

  • Warm White LED

    Soft, warm light built for evenings outside, not a security-light glare.

  • One Transformer, Up to 18 Lights

    A single low-voltage transformer runs a whole run of lights around steps, posts, and the deck perimeter.

  • 2-Year Warranty

    IP67-rated and built for the British weather, backed by a 2-year manufacturer warranty.
