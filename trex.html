From first chat to finished deck.

  1. Start the conversation

    Message me on WhatsApp, or leave your details and I'll call you. I'll talk through what you're picturing before anything else.

  2. A good fit

    Making sure we're a good fit. We like to see your project and ask the right questions, to make sure what we offer is right for you. We can send you samples and get a rough size and design brief, so we can give you a budget expectation.

  3. Survey and fixed quotation

    Depending on our first call, we may skip step 2. During our meeting I'll make sure the design suits your needs and meets the manufacturer's requirements, delivering the best value for your investment. I'll help you make the right product choices, and I always give a written quotation at this point.

  4. Booking in

    We require a deposit before booking dates. At this point all materials are ordered and kept in stock at our yard, ready for your build date, for a smooth, hassle-free build.

  5. The build

    Built to the manufacturer's requirements, on UC4 pressure-treated joists with Trex Protect joist tape, and plastic subframes on a concrete raft for ground-level decks.

  6. Handover

    A full walkthrough, your warranty documentation, and care instructions for the years ahead.

Common questions.

Which range is right for me?

It depends on the look you're after more than anything else. Enhance for a natural-wood look. Transcend for the richest, deepest colours and grain. Signature for a clean, architectural finish on a contemporary home. I'll talk it through properly on a site visit.

Can I get free samples before deciding?

Yes. Message me your name and address and I'll get real samples to you, so you can see the actual colour and grain in your own garden before committing to anything.

Why does the installer matter if the board is the same?

The board is only ever as good as what's underneath it. I use UC4 pressure-treated joists and Trex Protect joist tape as standard, with plastic subframes on a concrete raft for ground-level decks, exactly the kind of detail that decides whether a deck is still right in fifteen years or needs rebuilding.

How long does installation take?

Most residential decks take a few days to a couple of weeks, depending on size and complexity. I'll give you a precise timeline once I've seen the space.
