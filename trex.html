Common questions.

Which range is right for me?

It depends on the look you're after more than anything else. Enhance for a natural-wood look. Transcend for the richest, deepest colours and grain. Signature for a clean, architectural finish on a contemporary home. I'll talk it through properly on a site visit.

Can I get free samples before deciding?

Yes. Message me your name and address and I'll get real samples to you, so you can see the actual colour and grain in your own garden before committing to anything.

Why does the installer matter if the board is the same?

The board is only ever as good as what's underneath it. I use UC4-rated timber joists, joist protection tape, and plastic subframes instead of timber posts sunk into the ground, exactly the kind of detail that decides whether a deck is still right in fifteen years or needs rebuilding.

How long does installation take?

Most residential decks take a few days to a couple of weeks, depending on size and complexity. I'll give you a precise timeline once I've seen the space.
