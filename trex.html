Why the Installer Matters as Much as the Board

Trex builds the board. We build the deck that lasts.

We've been a TrexPro Platinum installer since 2017, the highest certification Trex awards, and in 2024 became a member of Trex's own Pro Advisory Council. Every deck we build uses UC4-rated timber joists, joist protection tape, and plastic subframes rather than timber posts sunk in the ground. That's the difference between a deck that needs attention in ten years and one that doesn't.
